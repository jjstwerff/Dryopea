Lore
Faster than light travel and communication is possible through unstable and relatively small wormholes. Those have a high energy cost to create and are only stable far outside gravitational holes and need to be detected again after one closes.
Bulk transport is limited to slower than light transports. But in emergencies a wormhole from outer space can be made.

The planet has aggressive pollen, against humans and machines. The local fauna adapted.
Trees continue to grow for millennia but not straight up. Huge trunks can grow horizontal.
Spikes on moving branches that can sweep back quickly.
Sticky surfaces.
Thick and strong bark. Minerals on the surface.
Acid fruits and pools.
Aggressive sticky pollen.
Animals living in symbiosis with the trees. Possibly even shamans.

This gives an indication that the AI is already repairing their machines.
There were already humans living on this planet with difficulty with the wildlife.
They crash landed on the planet when their generation ship missed their original target.

They used small entrances as a way to be safe against most of them. Used rock paintings.
A brilliant female hacked the surveillance computer to prevent universal discovery.
Another added hostility and unbridled growth to it as a means to control the others. This failed and the one responsible is punished for it.

They are still there living in caves and trees outside of the mining opportunities of the AI.
They have bonded with the living roots of the planet's trees in shamanistic fashion.
Can communicate with the trees, at least detect threats in different areas. They use this to keep out of reach of AI and players.
Some of them still scrounge the surface with special suits.
Lately they mostly take things from broken AI robots. But also animal skins and meat.
The rockets into the sky are a cry of help from yet another human. This is not the AI's doing.

Trees also grow horizontally and have a sap that can create super concrete. It can also be burned or distilled into batteries. They live in symbiosis with ants that live on concentrated sap that is even more energetic. Those ants can feel emergency signals of the tree and have an odor sack in them that attracts and alerts all kinds of animals. Trees can grow potent fruit to procreate and can have one or more hearts to store valuable minerals or water under dry circumstances.

The trees can float in the ocean but do not like to be submerged in fresh water. They have limited control over the shape of their trunks. They can create deep tunnels to get to deep minerals and eventually back out again after depletion.

Mission
Perspective:
Individual humans can have skills and different pool levels. So they need rest/sleep.
Always show the world from the perspective of a human.
Possible to switch to another human when needed. Though there needs to be an inherent cost to prevent steering multiple units at the same time. Only switching to a human that is at the main communication terminal. So there is a clear reason to want to return there.

Take over or dismantle an installation. Like a mine or production facility or launch spot.

Valuable artifacts: human settlers, competitors, artistic items, local flora & fauna.
Interesting materials: laser lenses, diamonds, AI-cores, unique tree sap & seeds.
Terrain:
Any location should give reasons to build extensive structures. It is vital to create compact installations but impossible to do it in a single spot and get the mission objectives.

Stealth is possible however not for the jammer. Construction could be with low visibility.
Later developments allow for a flier instead of a speeder. However it has to be much lighter to function and has limited fuel.
Land with a rocket on a chosen spot. Directly functions as a jammer and emergency vehicle.
Limited materials in weight and volume. Humans that take time to revive.

Only progression outside missions by launching rockets to space.

It is possible to mine every material but harvesting robots is more efficient.
